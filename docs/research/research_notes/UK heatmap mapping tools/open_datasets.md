# Open UK datasets for a £/m² property map: download specifics, formats, licences and join keys (as of 10 October 2026)

Scope: England & Wales primary; Scotland and Northern Ireland noted separately. All URLs, sizes and dates below were checked on 10 October 2026 unless stated. Where I verified a file directly with `curl` (HTTP HEAD / partial GET / row count) I say so; those checks are first-hand observations rather than page quotations.

## Quick answers to the five key questions

### Takeaway
PPD does NOT carry UPRN in its own columns; since 28 August 2026 HM Land Registry publishes a separate monthly "Transaction unique identifier ↔ UPRN" lookup for new transactions only (no back-dating). EPC open data has carried UPRN since November 2021 and coverage rose from ~92% (end-2021 release) to ~97.8% (mid-2022 release). Every download is anonymous except EPC bulk/API, which needs a GOV.UK One Login account. Realistic PPD↔EPC match rates: ~79% of all sales via address normalisation over long histories (Powell-Smith 2017; UCL 2011–2019 after cleaning), >90% per year for 2011 onwards, and ~96% via a UPRN rules-based route (UBDC 2026).

### Cited Findings
- PPD column list contains no UPRN; "No mention of UPRN appears on the page." — [About the Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/about-the-price-paid-data)
- UPRN and INSPIRE ID lookup tables "will apply to monthly Price Paid Data published from 28 August 2026 onwards" and "will not be available for previously published datasets." (news item dated 26 August 2026) — [HM Land Registry news](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/hm-land-registry-to-provide-property-identifiers-for-price-paid-data-from-28-august)
- The UPRN lookup "will not provide back-dated data"; only new price paid records are included. — [Technical specification: UPRN look up table](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/technical-specification-transaction-unique-identifier-and-uprn-look-up-table-dataset)
- EPC: "November 2021 – UPRNs (Unique Property Reference Numbers) were added to the data." — [EPC guidance: changes to the format and methodology](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/changes-to-the-format-and-methodology)
- EPC UPRN coverage 92.4% in the release to end-2021 (7.7% assessor-entered, 84.7% address-matched, 7.6% none); 97.8% in the release to end of June 2022. — [Owen Boswarva, 28 Mar 2022](https://owenboswarva.com/blog/post-hou4.htm); [Owen Boswarva, 16 Sep 2022](https://owenboswarva.com/blog/post-hou6.htm)
- Powell-Smith (2017): joining each sale to the property's most recent EPC using normalised addresses "finds a match 79% of the time, for around 6.2 million property sales." — [houseprices.anna.ps (Wayback Machine capture, 2019)](http://web.archive.org/web/20190409053622/http://houseprices.anna.ps/)
- UCL (Chi et al.): match rate "between 2011 and 2019 is higher than 90%"; "The match rate of 56.20% in 2008 is particularly low but rapidly increases to over 88% after 2010"; final cleaned dataset "represents 79.11% of full market property sales in the LR-PPD in England and Wales between 1/1/2011 and 31/10/2019." — [UCL Open: Environment preprint PDF](https://journals.uclpress.co.uk/ucloepreprints/repository/object/229/download/82)
- UBDC/Glasgow (dataset dated 27 Jan 2026): EPC-to-UPRN linkage "had a 96% match rate", versus DLUHC's own 93%; the same 96% was achievable for Price Paid Data with a rules-based method. — [UBDC EPC-to-UPRN lookup record](https://researchdata.gla.ac.uk/2211/); [UBDC news](https://ubdc.ac.uk/news/key-solutions-to-unlock-the-power-of-location-data)

### Inferences
- For any sale before August 2026 the official UPRN lookup does not help; a UPRN route for history requires address-matching PPD to a UPRN-bearing address source. There is no open address→UPRN gazetteer for England & Wales (OS Open UPRN has coordinates only; AddressBase/NGD are licensed), so in practice the EPC file itself (address + UPRN) is the open bridge.
- From the August 2026 monthly PPD file (90,867 rows, my count) and the August 2026 lookup (84,148 rows, my count), roughly 92–93% of newly published transactions receive a UPRN. This is an approximation: the monthly file also contains Change/Delete rows, and the lookup may include rows for transactions not in that month's file.

### Gaps
- HM Land Registry publishes no coverage percentage for its UPRN lookup; the figure above is my own ratio.
- No official MHCLG statement of the current (2026) EPC UPRN coverage was found; the 97.8% figure is a 2022 third-party measurement. The March 2025 switch to OS NGD address matching was reported to reduce missing UPRNs but the news page could not be fetched (TLS certificate error) so this is from search-result snippets only.

## (1) HM Land Registry Price Paid Data (PPD)

### Takeaway
PPD is a header-less CSV/TXT with 16 fields (15 in the complete file plus Record Status in the monthly file), covering England & Wales sales from 1 January 1995, updated on the 20th working day each month under OGL v3 with a mandatory HMLR attribution; the complete CSV is ~5.5 GB and the monthly update ~16 MB. A SPARQL endpoint is live, and since 28 Aug 2026 a monthly UPRN lookup accompanies each release.

### Cited Findings
Download URLs, sizes and dates
- Landing pages: downloads index, single file, yearly file, UPRN lookup, INSPIRE ID lookup. "We update the data on the 20th working day of each month." Index last updated 29 September 2026 "with information from August 2026." — [Price Paid Data downloads](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-downloads)
- Complete file: `https://price-paid-data.publicdata.landregistry.gov.uk/pp-complete.csv` (page says 5.3 GB) and `.../pp-complete.txt` (5.1 GB); "August data added", page updated 1 October 2026. — [Price Paid Data: single file](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-single-file)
- Verified by HTTP HEAD on 10 Oct 2026: `pp-complete.csv` Content-Length 5,525,539,417 bytes, Last-Modified Mon 28 Sep 2026 05:12 GMT (first-hand check).
- Monthly update: `https://price-paid-data.publicdata.landregistry.gov.uk/pp-monthly-update-new-version.csv` (17.9 MB per page) and `.../pp-monthly-update.txt` (17.3 MB); published 29 September 2026; "the first release of data for August 2026 (transactions received from the first to the last day of the month)". — [Price Paid Data: August 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-august-2026)
- Verified: `pp-monthly-update-new-version.csv` Content-Length 15,840,574 bytes, Last-Modified 28 Sep 2026; 90,867 rows; first row observed: `"{5A7B802C-4C8F-1886-E063-4704A8C021AE}","428000","2021-07-31 00:00","NW7 1SN","F","Y","L","BARTRAM HOUSE, 10","FLAT 29","MAURICE BROWNE AVENUE","","LONDON","BARNET","GREATER LONDON","A","A"` — note the monthly file contains transactions with much older transfer dates (late registrations/changes), every field double-quoted, date as `YYYY-MM-DD 00:00`, no header row (first-hand check).
- Yearly files: "the sizes of these files range from 115 MB to 230 MB"; split halves are offered. — [Price Paid Data downloads](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-downloads)
- No login or API key is required for any PPD file (all fetched anonymously with curl; first-hand).

Column schema (no header row; order as published)
- 1 Transaction unique identifier ("will change each time a sale is recorded"); 2 Price; 3 Date of Transfer; 4 Postcode (as at the time of the transaction; reallocated postcodes not reflected); 5 Property Type D/S/T/F/O; 6 Old/New Y/N; 7 Duration F/L (leases of 7 years or less not recorded); 8 PAON; 9 SAON; 10 Street; 11 Locality; 12 Town/City; 13 District; 14 County; 15 PPD Category Type A (standard) / B (additional: power-of-sale, repossessions, buy-to-lets identifiable by mortgage, transfers to non-private individuals, "Other" property types); 16 Record Status (monthly file only) A = Addition, C = Change, D = Delete. — [About the Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/about-the-price-paid-data)
- Category A collected from January 1995; Category B identified from October 2013; a category change is handled as a delete plus a re-add under a new transaction identifier. — [About the Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/about-the-price-paid-data)
- Exclusions: sales not lodged, not for value, discounted right-to-buy, transfers subject to an existing mortgage, share transfers, gifts, CPOs, court orders, transfers to trustees, multi-property vesting deeds/assents; VAT-inclusive consideration excluded. Registration lags sale by "between 2 weeks and 2 months", so the two most recent months are incomplete. — [About the Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/about-the-price-paid-data)
- Known quality caveats: no bungalow/end-terrace distinction; Locality inconsistently populated (search by Town); postcode and district/county changes not applied retrospectively; the same address can appear with different property types over time; Excel mangles dates. — [About the Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/about-the-price-paid-data)

Linked data / SPARQL
- Linked-data entry point and report builder: `https://landregistry.data.gov.uk/#linked-data`, `http://landregistry.data.gov.uk/app/ppd`. — [Price Paid Data downloads](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-downloads)
- SPARQL endpoint `https://landregistry.data.gov.uk/landregistry/query` is live: a GET with `Accept: application/sparql-results+json` and `SELECT ?s WHERE {?s a <http://landregistry.data.gov.uk/def/ppi/TransactionRecord>} LIMIT 1` returned `http://landregistry.data.gov.uk/data/ppi/transaction/702EF0D1-.../current` on 10 Oct 2026 (first-hand check). Third-party docs describe the same endpoint and prefix `ppd: <http://landregistry.data.gov.uk/def/ppi/>` with `output=json`. — [mcpbundles HM Land Registry skill](https://www.mcpbundles.com/skills/hm-land-registry-price-paid); historical description of the Fuseki-based endpoint — [Leigh Dodds, 2013](https://blog.ldodds.com/2013/01/29/a-brief-review-of-the-land-registry-linked-data/)
- R client `uklr` wraps the SPARQL endpoint for PPD, transactions and HPI. — [uklr on CRAN](https://search.r-project.org/CRAN/packages/uklr/uklr.pdf)

PPD-to-UPRN lookup
- Dataset: "Transaction unique identifier and UPRN Look Up Table"; files `https://price-paid-data.publicdata.landregistry.gov.uk/pp-uprn-lookup-aug-2026.csv` and `.txt` (about 4 MB each); first published 28 August 2026, updated 29 September 2026; monthly on the 20th working day; two columns (Transaction unique identifier, UPRN). — [UPRN look up table dataset](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/transaction-unique-identifier-and-uprn-look-up-table-dataset)
- Verified: the August 2026 CSV is 4,624,174 bytes, 84,148 rows, no header, both fields double-quoted, e.g. `"{326EFAEB-0447-475E-8A3A-02EE17C340BE}","200001854975"` (first-hand check).
- Matching basis: "Transactions can only be linked to a UPRN where an Ordnance Survey AddressBase address relationship exists with the transaction identifier"; HMLR uses OS AddressBase Premium with Royal Mail PAF to link address/Title Number to UPRN; a UPRN is included only where a reliable link exists; AddressBase Premium is published every six weeks. — [Technical specification](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/technical-specification-transaction-unique-identifier-and-uprn-look-up-table-dataset)
- Companion INSPIRE ID lookup exists at the same cadence. — [INSPIRE ID look up table](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/transaction-unique-identifier-and-inspire-id-look-up-table-dataset)
- UPRNs are created by local authorities and curated by GeoPlace, then shared with Ordnance Survey. — [HM Land Registry news, 26 Aug 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/hm-land-registry-to-provide-property-identifiers-for-price-paid-data-from-28-august)

Licence and attribution (verbatim strings)
- PPD attribution: "Contains HM Land Registry data © Crown copyright and database right 2021. This data is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." — [Price Paid Data downloads](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-downloads)
- Third-party rights: OGL does not cover the Address Data (Royal Mail and OS). Address Data use is permitted "for personal and/or non-commercial use" and for "displaying residential property price information services"; other uses require contacting Royal Mail at address.management@royalmail.com. — [Price Paid Data: single file](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/price-paid-data-single-file)
- UPRN lookup attribution: "Contains HM Land Registry data © Crown copyright and database right [year of supply or date of publication]. This data is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." and "UPRNs contain OS data © Crown copyright and database rights [year of supply or date of publication]. This data is licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0." — [UPRN look up table dataset](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistical-data-sets/transaction-unique-identifier-and-uprn-look-up-table-dataset)
- Users must ensure use does not breach the Data Protection Act 2018 or PECR 2003. — [About the Price Paid Data](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/about-the-price-paid-data)

### Inferences
- A public £/m² map that shows aggregated prices (not a mailing/marketing use) falls within the explicitly permitted "displaying residential property price information services" use of the address fields, but the HMLR attribution string must be shown verbatim and the Royal Mail/OS rights acknowledged.
- The transaction identifier changes when a record is re-published (category change = delete + add), so joins between the UPRN lookup and the complete file must be refreshed monthly rather than cached once.
- The monthly file includes old-dated transactions and C/D rows; a pipeline should apply A/C/D status against the complete file rather than appending.

### Gaps
- No official statement of what fraction of PPD transactions receive a UPRN.
- The technical specification's file-naming pattern (`pp-uprn-lookup_<Month><Year>.csv`) does not match the actual file name (`pp-uprn-lookup-aug-2026.csv`); treat the live link as authoritative.
- I did not find a current HMLR page documenting the SPARQL vocabulary; the 2013 blog is the fullest description.

## (2) EPC register open data (England & Wales, MHCLG)

### Takeaway
The service moved to `https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/` (beta); the old `epc.opendatacommunities.org/docs/*` URLs 301-redirect there. Bulk CSV and the API require a GOV.UK One Login account (bearer token); CSV downloads refresh on the 1st of each month, the API daily. UPRN has been included since November 2021 with coverage ~98% by mid-2022; in March 2026 `LMK_KEY` was removed in favour of `CERTIFICATE_NUMBER`. Non-address fields (including UPRN) are OGL v3; address fields carry OS/Royal Mail restrictions limited to listed energy-efficiency purposes.

### Cited Findings
Service, coverage, registration
- "This data covers England and Wales." Scotland has a separate register. Records are certificates registered since 2012 which "may have expired or been replaced." You must sign in or create a GOV.UK One Login account to download bulk data. — [Get energy performance of buildings data](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/)
- `https://epc.opendatacommunities.org/docs/guidance`, `/docs/api` and `/docs/copyright` all returned `301 Moved Permanently` to `https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/` on 10 Oct 2026 (first-hand check).
- Guidance sub-pages: `/guidance/data-dictionary`, `/guidance/linking-certificates-to-recommendations`, `/guidance/how-the-data-is-produced`, `/guidance/changes-to-the-format-and-methodology`, `/guidance/data-limitations`, `/guidance/licensing-restrictions`, `/guidance/data-protection-requirements`, `/guidance/energy-certificate-data-apis`, `/api-technical-documentation`. — [EPC guidance index](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance)
- "Data accessed via the CSV downloads is updated on the first day of each month." "Data accessed via a developer API is updated daily." — [How the data is produced](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/how-the-data-is-produced)
- Data limitations page says "Data has been published from 2012 onwards"; but the January 2024 service change added annual files "back to 2008", and the UCL work used EPCs lodged from 1 October 2008. — [Data limitations](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/data-limitations); [MHCLG Digital blog, 29 Jan 2024](https://mhclgdigital.blog.gov.uk/2024/01/29/changes-to-the-energy-performance-certificates-open-data-service); [UCL preprint](https://journals.uclpress.co.uk/ucloepreprints/repository/object/229/download/82)

Bulk download structure
- January 2024 changes: single-file downloads of all certificates and recommendations per certificate type; monthly files for the last 12 months and annual files back to 2008; some zips include `schema.json`; files have "a predictable, permanent URL"; API 5,000/10,000-record limits removed in favour of new pagination; OpenAPI v3 schemas published. — [MHCLG Digital blog, 29 Jan 2024](https://mhclgdigital.blog.gov.uk/2024/01/29/changes-to-the-energy-performance-certificates-open-data-service)
- Older per-local-authority structure: a certificates CSV, a recommendations CSV linked via `LMK_KEY`, and a licence text file; filtered multi-authority downloads capped at the first 5,000 results. — [Open Data Communities guide (last updated Dec 2021)](https://guides.opendatacommunities.org/article/40-energy-performance-certificates-download)
- Size indications (older): all-domestic bulk zip "around 3GB in size, and once unzipped is around 25GB" (University of Liverpool); single-LA zips "typically 5 to 50 MB" (ukhousing R package docs). — [University of Liverpool GDSL](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/geographic-data-science/news/stories/title,986085,en.php); [ukhousing R package](https://charlescoverdale.r-universe.dev/ukhousing/ukhousing.pdf)

API
- Base URL `https://api.get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk`; header `Authorization: Bearer <token>` where the token "can be found on your my account page when you are logged in to the service"; `Accept: application/json`; GET only; example `GET /api/certificate?certificate_number=1111-2222-3333-4444-5555`. — [Making a request](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/api-technical-documentation/making-a-request)
- "You need to sign in or create an account with GOV.UK One Login to receive a bearer token." — [Energy certificate data APIs](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/energy-certificate-data-apis)
- `GET /api/domestic/search` parameters: `postcode`, `uprn` (12 digits, zero-padded, e.g. `000001234567`), `address`, `council[]`, `constituency[]`, `efficiency_rating[]`, `date_start`, `date_end` (YYYY-MM-DD; at least one parameter required; cannot include today). Paginated above 5,000 records; page size 1–5,000 (`page`/`page_size` naming inconsistent in the docs); response has `data[]` (certificateNumber, addressLine1–4, postcode, postTown, council, constituency, currentEnergyEfficiencyBand, registrationDate, uprn, schemaType) and `pagination` (totalRecords, currentPage, totalPages, nextPage, prevPage, pageSize). 400 on bad params, 404 when nothing matches. — [Search certificates: domestic](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/api-technical-documentation/search-certificates/domestic)
- Rate limit: "Our standard limit is 6000 requests every 5 minutes per originating IP address"; HTTP 429 above that. Documentation also lists full-load download endpoints (`/api-technical-documentation/download/...`), changed-certificate/delta endpoints and an OpenAPI file at `/api-documentation/index.html`. — [API technical documentation](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/api-technical-documentation)

UPRN population
- End-2021 release: UPRNs on 20,551,337 of 22,243,396 records (92%); 1,716,164 assessor-supplied, 18,835,173 address-matched; "About 25% of the 15,678,307 unique UPRNs in the domestic EPC dataset are linked to more than one EPC record." — [Owen Boswarva, 28 Mar 2022](https://owenboswarva.com/blog/post-hou4.htm)
- Release to end June 2022: 97.8% of domestic EPCs have a UPRN; DLUHC added UPRNs to "more than 1.27 million EPC records that lacked UPRNs"; reasons for gaps: property no longer exists, incomplete/non-standard lodged address, UPRN not yet in AddressBase. Error examples: Flats A–E at one Reading address all share a UPRN belonging only to Flat D; a Hackney building with 90+ studio rooms all carry the building's single UPRN. — [Owen Boswarva, 16 Sep 2022](https://owenboswarva.com/blog/post-hou6.htm)
- Department's address-matching rule: a UPRN is assigned by algorithm only if the assessor did not submit one and the algorithm finds a reliable match; validation against OS/GeoPlace matched datasets found UPRNs matched "in up to 99% of cases." — [Open Data Communities: EPCs now include UPRN](https://news.opendatacommunities.org/energy-performance-certificates-now-include-uprn); [OS blog](https://ordnancesurvey.co.uk/blog/ordnance-survey-open-identifiers-uprn-epc-data-release)

Licence / copyright
- All fields except address and postcode fields (and including OS UPRNs) are licensed under OGL v3. Address fields (address, address1–3, postcode) are processed against OS AddressBase Premium incorporating Royal Mail PAF; OS and Royal Mail permit use only for listed purposes including managing properties to promote energy efficiency, research into building energy efficiency, evaluating energy-efficiency programmes, "promoting energy efficiency improvements and understanding building efficiency in sale or rental markets", enforcement, local-authority duties, crime prevention/legal proceedings; other uses need a licence (PricingLicensing@os.uk / address.management@royalmail.com). When sharing address data for a permitted purpose "you must include a copy of this copyright and database right notice with the data." — [Licensing restrictions](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/licensing-restrictions)
- Site-level: "All content is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise stated"; © Crown copyright. — [Get energy performance of buildings data](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/)

Known data-quality issues
- Multiple EPCs per building (expired/replaced certificates included); "should not be interpreted as a true representation of the whole of the building stock"; ~8,600 records with null local authority (address could not be matched to OS lookup tables); out-of-bounds values marked "INVALID"; non-mandatory fields may read "NO DATA!", "N/A", "Not recorded"; opt-outs and national-security withholdings excluded; LOCAL_AUTHORITY/CONSTITUENCY reflect current boundaries. — [Data limitations](https://get-energy-performance-data.communities.gov.uk/guidance/data-limitations)
- Floor-area cleaning in practice (UCL): records with NA/0 floor area or rooms, floor area <9 m² or >974 m², £/m² >50,000 or <200, >100 m² per habitable room, >20 rooms, <6.51 m² per room were removed; "accounts for 15.11% of the linked geo-referenced data" (1,020,469 of 6,753,307), 99.59% of exclusions being NA/0 values; example of "total floor area records as 0.01". — [UCL preprint](https://journals.uclpress.co.uk/ucloepreprints/repository/object/229/download/82)
- Measurement accuracy: a commercial report claims one in four EPCs mis-state size by more than 10%, average discrepancy 8.6%; academic work attributes errors to irregular rooms and the fact that ~90% of EPCs use RdSAP standardised values. — [The Construction Index](https://www.theconstructionindex.co.uk/news/view/mismeasurement-makes-15-of-epc-ratings-wrong); [Showhouse](https://www.showhouse.co.uk/?p=80384)
- Irish national EPC database study: 30% of entries erroneous/outliers, 80% of those geometric; suggests bounds by building type/age. — [MaREI](https://www.marei.ie/a-new-paper-introduces-the-first-automated-data-driven-validation-of-an-epc-dataset/)

### Inferences
- Any pipeline keyed on `LMK_KEY` broke in March 2026; use `CERTIFICATE_NUMBER` and expect no bridge from old keys.
- A £/m² map arguably falls under "understanding building efficiency in sale or rental markets" only if energy efficiency is part of the use; the safer reading is that address fields may be used internally for matching, while the published layer should expose aggregated values or UPRN/coordinates rather than EPC address strings. This is a legal-interpretation inference, not a documented position.
- For deduplication, keep the latest `LODGEMENT_DATETIME` per UPRN (or per normalised address) and prefer the EPC nearest the sale date for floor area, as UCL did.

### Gaps
- I could not reach the signed-in bulk-download page, so current zip sizes, per-LA availability and whether `schema.json` is still shipped are unverified for the new domain (`/api-technical-documentation/download-info/domestic` returned 404 anonymously).
- No 2025–2026 official figure for the share of records with UPRN after the NGD switch.
- The two-stated start dates (2008 vs 2012) are not reconciled on the new site.

### Inferences

- Join key PPD→HPI: PPD `District` text must be mapped to an ONS GSS code (via ONSPD/NSPL `oslaua` or a name lookup) because PPD has no codes and district names changed with 2019–2023 local-government reorganisations.

## (7) Address matching between PPD and EPC: approaches, match rates and libraries

### Takeaway
Documented address-normalisation approaches reach ~79% of all sales over long periods (Powell-Smith 2017; UCL 2011–2019 after cleaning) and >90% per year from 2011 (UCL, 251-rule four-stage matcher), with flats, City of London and Isles of Scilly the weak spots; a UPRN rules-based route achieves ~96% on both EPC and PPD (UBDC 2026). The practical open-source toolkit is Splink and the Splink-based `uk_address_matcher` (DuckDB, MIT, ~100k addresses in 30 s); libpostal appears in matching frameworks (PostMatch) but no PPD–EPC project using it was found.

### Cited Findings
- Powell-Smith (2017 map, archived): "Sale prices taken from Land Registry's Price Paid dataset of residential property sales to individuals since August 2007. Floor area in m2 per property taken from Energy Performance Certificates. I join each property sale to the property's most recent EPC, using normalised addresses: this finds a match 79% of the time, for around 6.2 million property sales. The aggregate price per m2 for each postcode district is then calculated as the total price of all sales, divided by the total floor area of all properties." Attribution block used: "Postcode district derived boundaries reproduced under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Postal Boundaries © GeoLytix copyright and database right 2012. Contains Ordnance Survey data © Crown copyright and database right 2012. Contains Royal Mail data © Royal Mail copyright and database right 2012. Contains National Statistics data © Crown copyright and database right 2012. Price Paid data produced by HM Land Registry © Crown copyright 2017." — [houseprices.anna.ps via Wayback Machine (2019 capture)](http://web.archive.org/web/20190409053622/http://houseprices.anna.ps/); the live site now returns a GitHub Pages 404 (first-hand check) and her homepage summarises it as "6.2 million house sales" in 2017 — [anna.ps](https://anna.ps/)

### Inferences
- Realistic expectations for a new build: ~75–80% of all E&W sales since 2008 matched by address normalisation, rising to 90%+ for sales from 2011 onward; a UPRN-based route is only possible going forward (August 2026+) via the HMLR lookup, or historically by first matching PPD addresses to EPC addresses (which then supply the UPRN), so the "UPRN route" for history is still an address match in disguise and should land in the same 90–96% band for well-formed house addresses, lower for flats.
- Recommended key design: block on postcode (PPD `Postcode` ↔ EPC `POSTCODE`), compare normalised `PAON/SAON/Street` against EPC `ADDRESS1–3`, resolve 1:n by nearest `LODGEMENT_DATE` to `Date of Transfer` with non-null `TOTAL_FLOOR_AREA`, then carry EPC `UPRN` to join OS Open UPRN coordinates; from August 2026 prefer the HMLR UPRN lookup for new transactions and reconcile against the address match.
- Floor-area sanity filters that the UCL team applied (9–974 m², 200–50,000 £/m², 6.51–100 m² per habitable room, ≤20 rooms) are a reasonable published baseline.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 blog post or paper giving a head-to-head PPD↔EPC match rate for UPRN joins versus address normalisation on the same corpus was found; the UBDC 96% figure is for UPRN assignment per dataset, not for the PPD↔EPC join itself.
- No documented use of `address-net` or `dedupe` for PPD–EPC matching was found.
- The peer-reviewed UCL article HTML (journals.uclpress.co.uk/ucloe) is behind bot protection; figures above come from the preprint PDF, which may differ slightly from the final version.
